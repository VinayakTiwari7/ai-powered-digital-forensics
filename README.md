# AI-Powered Digital Forensics

### Opportunities, Challenges, and the Future of Cybercrime Investigation

**Author:** Vinayak Tiwari  
**Domain:** Advanced Digital Forensics  
**Project type:** Self-learning technical article

## Overview

Artificial intelligence is changing how large volumes of digital information can be examined during cybercrime investigations. Techniques such as machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and anomaly detection can help investigators identify patterns and prioritise potentially relevant evidence.

However, AI-assisted analysis also introduces challenges involving accuracy, bias, privacy, transparency, and evidence integrity.

This repository explores the opportunities and limitations of AI in digital forensics, with an emphasis on responsible implementation and human oversight.

## Key Topics

- AI-assisted digital evidence triage
- Natural language processing and communication analysis
- Anomaly detection in system and network logs
- Evidence integrity and chain of custody
- False positives, false negatives, and AI-generated inaccuracies
- Privacy, security, and responsible AI use
- Human verification and reproducible forensic analysis
